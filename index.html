Welcome to Holy Ground

We are a husband and wife working together to build a place where people in need can find food, hope, community, and the love of Jesus Christ.

Our dream is to create a self-sustaining farm and community where people can work, grow food, share meals, find temporary shelter, and discover hope.

Our Story

We believe God has given us a vision for something bigger than ourselves. We want to use what we have to serve others and create a place where people are welcomed, loved, and fed.

This is the beginning of our journey.

Our Mission

  • Provide food for people in need.
  • Create opportunities for people to work and learn.
  • Build temporary housing for those who need it.
  • Create a place of prayer and Christian fellowship.
  • Grow food using sustainable farming practices.
  • Share the message and love of Jesus Christ.

The Farm

Our vision includes a working farm where fruits, vegetables, herbs, and other food can be grown to help feed the community.

As this dream grows, we hope to add animals, gardens, community spaces, and other resources that can help people become more self-sufficient.
